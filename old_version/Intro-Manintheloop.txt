0. Who we are
info.nodes is an Italian nonprofit organization, founded
in 2019 by journalists and activists to experiment new
solutions in the Italian civil society and media
landscape.
We focus on social and climate justice, the impact of
new technologies on human rights, and the protection
of freedom of speech.
We produce investigations, connect tech-activists,
researchers, and artists, develop innovative advocacy
and communication formats to reach wider audiences.
We also organize exhibitions, networking events, offer
technical training for journalists and activists, and
create investigative-based comms materials.
We love to work in collaboration with other
organizations, because “no man is an island”.
Project team: Davide Del Monte, Laura Carrer, Andrea
Daniele Signorelli

We want to highlight the significant growth
of the European startup sector dedicated to
the development of autonomous systems
and defense technologies.

As mentioned, one of the pieces of
information we are most interested in is
which funds, other companies, or
individuals are investing in the sector.
Ideally, we would be able to determine if
certain entities are investing across
multiple segments of the supply chain,
thereby securing a position of not just
economic advantage, but of decisionmaking power.
If an entity is present at every stage of
the chain (raw material extraction →
development of AI-based technologies
→ production of armaments using such
technology), it is evident that their power
increases considerably.

Regarding the startup sector in particular,
we also want to understand the role
played by European institutions,
specifically in financially backing and thus
promoting the development of this
industrial sector.
At this stage, we are mapping the various
European funds and programs directing
capital toward this area.
Starting from April, we plan to collect all
data on funding, projects, and
beneficiaries from the various European
portals.

4. The research

1.
Identification of companies

a) We created a test list of companies based
on market capitalization or economic valuation
(for unlisted companies, such as startups).
Additionally, we asked several Italian and
international organizations to suggest names
of companies they considered interesting for
inclusion in the list.
The list is actually composed by:
• 18 European start-up companies
• 22 Global Tech companies
• 65 Global Defence companies
• 67 Global Mining companies

b) Enrichment of the list of companies with
all the subjects (companies, universities,
research centers) that received European
funds (ex. EDF, Horizon, etc…)

4. The research

2.
Data collection

a) In mid-February, we opened a one-month
paid subscription on Crunchbase, one of the
most well-known private databases for
information on companies and investment
funds. This subscription allowed us to upload
our list of companies, view detailed
information on them, and export the relevant
data for our work in CSV format.
*Unfortunately, a direct API connection is not

possible, as the cost exceeds 35,000 USD per
year.

b) Datapitch developed a script to create a
GitHub-hosted mini-site for info.nodes.
The site lists every company previously
identified and includes dedicated pages that
organize their Wikipedia data according to a
custom schema we designed.
Connect -> Wikidata Inspector
